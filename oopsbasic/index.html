Logo exploration

VeoLMS — three directions

Your current mark is a friendly generic mascot (octopus) — it doesn't tell anyone this is a course marketplace. Each concept below builds the icon out of something literal to the product: a play control, an open book, or a progress path — so the mark still reads as "learning platform" even with the wordmark covered up.

01 · PlayCap
Mortarboard + play control
App icon / favicon at 24px — still reads clean
The diamond is a top-down graduation cap — instant "education" signal — but the notch cut into it is a play triangle, so it also reads as a course thumbnail. The small dot on the corner stands in for the tassel, but doubles as a "live / new course" indicator you could reuse in the product UI (notification dots, status badges).
02 · OpenPlay
Open book folding into a play button
Left page = content/curriculum, right = the play control
Left half is a book page (what the teacher builds), right half is a play triangle (what the student watches) — the whole icon is literally "course becomes video." It's the most narratively direct concept and the easiest to explain in a pitch deck, but it needs more negative space than the other two, so it's the trickiest to shrink to a 16px favicon.
03 · ProgressV
Monogram "V" as a completion path
Simplest silhouette of the three — reads at any size
A clean "V" for Veo, but drawn as one continuous stroke like a progress bar, ending in a dot that lands like a checkpoint or "course completed" marker. No literal book/cap/play iconography, so it ages better and won't box you into education-only visual clichés if the product expands — but it also relies more on the wordmark to carry the "learning" meaning on its own.

Kept the indigo→violet gradient DNA from your current logo (so it doesn't feel like a total rebrand) but swapped the orange for a single warm amber accent — reserved only for the "action" element in each mark (the play triangle, the checkpoint dot). That gives you one consistent color to mean "watch / buy / act" across the whole product, not just the logo.
My pick for a marketplace like this: Concept 02 (OpenPlay) if you want the icon to sell the product on its own — great for App Store / landing page. Concept 01 (PlayCap) if you want something that still feels a bit like your current mascot-driven identity. Concept 03 (ProgressV) if you want the most scalable, long-term brand mark.

These are SVG — fully vector, so a designer (or I) can hand you production files (favicon, app icon, dark/light lockups) in any size once you pick a direction.
